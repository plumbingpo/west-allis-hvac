Food Service Comfort

Comprehensive Food-Service HVAC Systems

Few buildings work an HVAC system harder than a restaurant. Every burner, fryer, and oven adds heat, the exhaust hoods draw conditioned air outside nonstop, and the makeup air unit has to replace that air without throwing off the building's pressure. West Allis HVAC services, sizes, and repairs the rooftop units, split systems, and makeup air equipment that keep the kitchen and dining room comfortable, and we schedule visits around your hours.

Air balance is where many restaurant HVAC problems start. When a kitchen runs under negative pressure, it pulls dining-room air in the wrong direction, drags hot or cold outdoor air in through every door, and wears out your staff at peak service. We balance the makeup air to the hood exhaust and log every visit in written reports your landlord and auditors can review.

Equipment replacements and maintenance schedules are planned with owners, managers, and kitchen leads, so everything fits your operating schedule. If a job can't be done during service, we install during off-hours, keeping your kitchen cooking and guests comfortable in the meantime.

Inspections & Documentation

Safe Installations, Documented Service

Commercial property owners have a duty of care to maintain safe, comfortable conditions for employees, customers, and tenants. A poorly installed or neglected HVAC system can result in carbon monoxide hazards, poor indoor air quality, refrigerant leaks, and costly water damage from clogged condensate lines. West Allis HVAC provides commercial installations and inspections that cover heat exchanger integrity, proper venting and combustion air, electrical connections and disconnects, refrigerant charge verification, condensate drainage protection, and ventilation rates for occupied spaces. Our written reports identify risks, assign priority levels, and recommend specific preventive actions.

Regular professional service protects your people and your property, and acting on expert recommendations keeps minor issues from becoming liabilities. It also creates a record. We maintain service logs, temperature and filter records, and a binder of reports for every account, and we increase visit frequency for heavy-use equipment in kitchens, gyms, and large mechanical rooms. When a landlord, insurer, or auditor asks for proof of due diligence, it's ready.

Fast Response

Fast Commercial HVAC Service

An HVAC emergency on a commercial property can shut down a kitchen, empty a retail floor on a summer afternoon, overheat a server room, and expose the owner to tenant disputes. West Allis HVAC provides fast, emergency HVAC service for commercial properties throughout West Allis and surrounding areas. When you call our commercial line, we dispatch an experienced technician quickly, because every hour without climate control costs you money.

Commercial emergencies come in many forms. The cause could be a failed compressor, a burned-out condenser fan motor, a refrigerant leak, an ignition or gas valve fault, a tripped high-limit switch, a frozen coil, or a control board stuck in lockout. Whatever the cause, we find it fast, get the building comfortable again, and document the entire call for your facility files. Then we put maintenance and monitoring in place to keep it from happening again.

Maintenance Programs

Commercial Scheduled Maintenance Programs

West Allis HVAC offers customized scheduled maintenance programs for commercial properties of all types and sizes. Scheduled maintenance typically costs a fraction of an emergency replacement and significantly reduces the risk of downtime and tenant complaints.
